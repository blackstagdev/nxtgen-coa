# Product / template mapping

_Generated from `app/src/coa/definitions/` by `npx tsx scripts/gen-mapping-doc.ts`. Do not edit by hand._

All blank and completed images are **1414 × 2000 px** (A4 portrait ≈ 171 dpi). Coordinates are pixels on the blank templates (copied to `app/assets/templates/<slug>/blank-pN.png`); completed examples are in `docs/reference/<slug>/filled-pN.png`. Text is Tinos (Times-metric) at 28 px for headers, 23.5 px for the Endotoxin/Sterility grids.

Signatures, signer blocks, procedures, conclusions, method lines and the footer are **pre-printed** on the blanks and are never generated. No values from the completed examples are used as defaults.

## Shared COA metadata

Fields used by more than one test type: client / client name, client email (pH, sterility), sample name, lot #, report date, test-article received, number of articles tested, test start/end dates. The **verification key** (`NGA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) is generated by the app and printed in every template's “Verification Key” box (large box on HPLC/MS and visual page 2; small box on Endotoxin, pH and ATP page 2).
